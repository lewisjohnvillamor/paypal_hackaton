Remedies

What you can offer instead of a full refund. When a buyer reports damage, AI compares their photos with each active remedy's "when it fits" description, and only the ones that fit go on the list you approve. A full refund is always one of the choices. When nothing here fits, the AI can suggest one of your other store listings on the case itself, and you decide whether to add it.
