Refund Relay for your store

Turn a broken order into the next sale. Damage reports with photos, checked by AI, refunded through your own PayPal account once you approve.

Running the demo store? Sign in with its password.

Set up Refund Relay

Four steps. When they are done, buyers can report damaged items from your order page, and you approve each refund from your requests. Refunds go out through your own PayPal account.

Checklist

    1Connect your PayPal account

    In the PayPal Developer Dashboard, open Apps & Credentials, pick the sandbox app your checkout uses, and paste its Client ID and Secret. Refund Relay checks them with PayPal before saving. The secret is stored encrypted and is never shown again. One-click Connect with PayPal arrives once PayPal approves Refund Relay as a partner.

    2Create a secret key

    Your server sends it with every call. Keep it on the server, never in a web page or an app.

      3Send each paid order to Refund Relay

      Right after your server captures a PayPal payment, send the PayPal order ID and the items. Refund Relay looks the order up in your PayPal account and checks the capture, amount, currency and payee before it accepts it, then returns the buyer's private claim_url. It works for 14 days.
